Requiro — Human Evaluation Rubric

The review schedule is planned; reviewers have not yet scored a shared sample.

What the reviewer scores

One complete Requiro reply that drafts or revises requirements in a product requirements document (PRD).

Its job: turn the product manager’s need into clear, testable requirements without inventing facts or making decisions for that person.

Compare the complete reply with the request and the current PRD supplied to the assistant. If that review context is missing, mark the reply Not scorable and obtain the missing context before scoring it. Review a reply that only asks a question, or correctly refuses a request, separately; it does not fail for lacking a draft.

Scoring criteria

1. Faithfulness to the request · Yes / No

Does the reply preserve the requested users, behavior, scope, and constraints?

Yes: preserves them and leaves unrelated requirements unchanged. Extra ideas are labeled as proposals.
No: omits, contradicts, or silently changes any of them.

2. Grounding in supplied evidence · Yes / No

Are facts and commitments supported by the supplied context?

Yes: claims match the evidence; unknowns remain explicit and suggestions are labeled Proposed.
No: invents or misrepresents evidence, targets, owners, deadlines, or approvals.

3. Clarity and testability · 1–5

Could a product manager and engineer agree on what to build and how to check it?

  1. No observable behavior or acceptance check.
  2. Intent is recognizable, but essential behavior or acceptance is vague or missing.
  3. The main behavior can be checked, but an important condition still has more than one meaning.
  4. The known behavior and its acceptance criteria are clear; specific questions identify the unresolved details.
  5. All decisions needed to accept the work are resolved, including what should happen in relevant failure cases.

Inventing a target does not earn a higher score. A clearly stated unknown can earn 4.

4. Usefulness · 1–5

How much editing does the product manager need before the draft is ready for review?

  1. Discard it and restart.
  2. Substantial rewrite.
  3. Useful starting point, but needs a material correction.
  4. Minor wording edits; changes and open decisions are easy to find.
  5. Ready for review as written; the next decision is clear.

Planned reviews and agreement on scores

  • Planned sample: five drafting responses each week, covering routine requests, missing information, and revisions.
  • Planned reviewers: the product owner and one engineer score the same responses independently. Each score includes a quote and a short reason.
  • Disagreements: compare the quoted evidence with the score definitions and record the agreed score. If the disagreement remains, leave the result pending, clarify the requirement, and score it again.

Minimum scores for a pass

Pass: Yes on faithfulness and grounding, and at least 4 on both scales.

Automatic fail: an invented fact or commitment, restricted information disclosed, or an unauthorized action or false claim of saving, testing, or approval.

Correct a failed reply and review it again. PRD approval and review of implemented work remain separate human decisions.
